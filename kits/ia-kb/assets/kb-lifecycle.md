# Reconciliation and lifecycle

Record the source and its evidence standing: proposal, accepted decision, implemented source, executed test, visual review or runtime receipt. No one status substitutes for all of them.

Keep original source attribution after acceptance. Require project-policy decision evidence for acceptance, withdrawal or supersession. An IA task's self-declared closed status is not the runtime next consumer's evidence verdict.

Review exact final bytes and disclose stale/missing bodies, failed checks, unresolved references and unavailable evaluators. A new revision invalidates evidence only within its declared dependency/scope; retaining evidence needs an explicit reason.

Use a selected-view summary when readers cannot receive the canonical private body. Never expand audience or silently copy private knowledge into a public plugin. Organisation-wide KB custody is an account capability, not supplied by this kit.
